Ode to the Sun

Golden orb in morning's light,
Chasing shadows through the night,
Painting skies with hues so bright,
Bringing warmth with all your might.

You rise above the eastern hill,
The sleeping world begins to thrill,
With gentle rays that softly spill,
And winter's icy grip you kill.

At noon you stand so proud and high,
A blazing jewel in the sky,
Watching clouds go drifting by,
As birds on heated currents fly.

Your evening glow, a masterpiece,
As all the daily noises cease,
You grant the world a gentle peace,
And let the stars their light increase.

Through seasons four you faithfully burn,
With lessons that we all must learn,
That after darkness, light returns,
And new beginnings we discern.

So thank you, Sun, for all you do,
For skies of gold and oceans blue,
For life that springs from morning dew,
And every day that starts anew.
